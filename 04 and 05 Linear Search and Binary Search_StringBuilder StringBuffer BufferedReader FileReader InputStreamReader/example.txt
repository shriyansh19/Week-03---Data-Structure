Here are some key points about Roger Federer:

1. **Full Name**: Roger Federer
2. **Date of Birth**: August 8, 1981
3. **Place of Birth**: Basel, Switzerland
4. **Nationality**: Swiss
5. **Height**: 6 feet 1 inch (185 cm)
6. **Plays**: Right-handed (one-handed backhand)
7. **Career Span**: 1998–2022 (officially retired in September 2022)
8. **Grand Slam Titles**: 20 (3rd most in men's singles history as of October 2023)
   - Australian Open: 6 titles
   - French Open: 1 title
   - Wimbledon: 8 titles (most in history)
   - US Open: 5 titles
9. **Weeks at World No. 1**: 310 weeks (record until surpassed by Novak Djokovic)
10. **ATP Tour Titles**: 103 singles titles (2nd most in the Open Era)
11. **Olympic Achievements**:
    - Gold Medal in Doubles (2008 Beijing Olympics with Stan Wawrinka)
    - Silver Medal in Singles (2012 London Olympics)
12. **Davis Cup**: Helped Switzerland win the Davis Cup in 2014.
13. **Laver Cup**: Co-created the Laver Cup, a team tennis event, in 2017.
14. **Playing Style**: Known for his elegant and versatile playing style, exceptional footwork, and precise shot-making.
15. **Rivalries**: Famous rivalries with Rafael Nadal, Novak Djokovic, and Andy Murray.
16. **Philanthropy**: Founded the Roger Federer Foundation in 2003, focusing on education and sports for children in Africa and Switzerland.
17. **Awards and Honors**:
    - ATP Player of the Year: 5 times
    - Laureus World Sportsman of the Year: 5 times
    - Stefan Edberg Sportsmanship Award: 13 times (voted by fellow players)
18. **Retirement**: Announced retirement on September 15, 2022, after the Laver Cup.
19. **Legacy**: Widely regarded as one of the greatest tennis players of all time (GOAT) and a global ambassador for the sport.
20. **Personal Life**: Married to Mirka Federer (former tennis player), with two sets of twins: Myla and Charlene (born 2009), and Leo and Lenny (born 2014).
